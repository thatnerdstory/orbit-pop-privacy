Orbit Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Last updated: November 15, 2026

That Nerd Story ("we", "us", or "our") develops Orbit Pop, an Android arcade game with package name com.thatnerdstory.orbitpop. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game and contact us for support.

At a glance
Orbit Pop can be played offline and requires no account. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. The Android app uses Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads when available.

1. Information handled by the game

That Nerd Story does not operate a server that receives your game progress or preferences. Google may receive information through the advertising SDK as described below.

The game stores the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

  • Completed levels, unlocked levels, Endless wave records, and Boss Trial completion.
  • Star balance, earned level rewards, and the date used to award daily stars.
  • Tutorial completion and sound, music, and volume preferences.

These records are used to remember your progress and settings between sessions. They are not sent to That Nerd Story.

2. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

Orbit Pop uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK for AdMob. Rewarded ads are available when you choose to watch an ad for an in-game reward. Interstitial ads may appear after a level, wave, or phase is completed.

Depending on your device, settings, and applicable consent choices, the SDK can collect and share the following information with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time and app hangs.
  • Device or account identifiers where applicable, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads and detect invalid ad activity. Google may personalise ads depending on your choices and applicable requirements. How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google Privacy Policy.

Orbit Pop uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage ad privacy choices where applicable. When the Ad privacy choices button is available in the game's Settings, you can use it to review or change those choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings where supported. Manage Android ad privacy settings.

3. Device permissions and platform services

Orbit Pop uses the internet to request ads and manage ad privacy choices. The Android package includes the advertising ID permission through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Orbit Pop does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or device location permissions.

Android and Google Play may independently process information to provide app installation, updates, security, diagnostics, or device backup services under their own policies and your device settings. Android backup or device transfer may include locally stored app data. We do not receive or control those platform backups. You can manage available backup options through your device settings.

4. Support communications

If you email thatnerdstory@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Email is handled through our email provider, Gmail. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and maintain relevant support records, or as required by law. You may request deletion by emailing us. We will delete information we control unless retention is necessary to meet a legal obligation or resolve an ongoing dispute.

5. Storage, security, and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until they are reset or the app's storage is cleared. You can reset unlocked level progress through the game's Settings screen. To remove all locally stored game data, use Android's app settings to clear Orbit Pop's storage or uninstall the app. Android or your backup provider manages any device backups separately.

Your device's security and access controls help protect local records. Google encrypts data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK in transit. Google manages retention and deletion of data it collects through its services. Google's data retention policy.

6. Children's privacy

Orbit Pop does not require players to create an account or provide personal details to play. The advertising information described above may be processed when the game requests ads. If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy concern about a child's use of Orbit Pop, contact us so we can review your request.

7. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added cookies, advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts to this page. GitHub may process technical information about website visits, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Visiting this page is separate from playing Orbit Pop offline.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or our information practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised last-updated date.

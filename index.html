Orbit Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Last updated: October 27, 2026

That Nerd Story ("we", "us", or "our") develops Orbit Pop, an Android arcade game with package name com.thatnerdstory.orbitpop. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game and contact us for support.

At a glance: Orbit Pop works offline, requires no account, and does not send gameplay or personal information to us. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. The current version contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

1. Information handled by the game

Orbit Pop does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers or to third-party services. We do not operate a server that receives data from the game. The game does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, payment details, advertising identifier, or other device identifiers.

The game stores the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

  • Completed levels and unlocked level progress.
  • Saved score information.
  • Sound, music, and volume preferences.

These records are used to remember your progress and settings between sessions. They are not sent to That Nerd Story.

2. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

The current version of Orbit Pop does not display advertisements or include analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or share gameplay records or personal information from the game with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Device permissions and platform services

Orbit Pop does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location. Its Android package includes the internet permission used by its app framework, but the current game does not use it to transmit user data.

Android and Google Play may independently process information to provide app installation, updates, security, diagnostics, or device backup services under their own policies and your device settings. Android backup or device transfer may include locally stored app data. We do not receive or control those platform backups. You can manage available backup options through your device settings.

4. Support communications

If you email thatnerdstory@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Email is handled through our email provider, Gmail. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and maintain relevant support records, or as required by law. You may request deletion by emailing us. We will delete information we control unless retention is necessary to meet a legal obligation or resolve an ongoing dispute.

5. Storage, security, and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until they are reset or the app's storage is cleared. You can reset unlocked level progress through the game's Settings screen. To remove all locally stored game data, use Android's app settings to clear Orbit Pop's storage or uninstall the app. This removes saved progress and preferences from that installation; device backups, if enabled, are managed separately through Android or your backup provider.

We cannot access or restore your local game records. Your device's security and access controls help protect those records. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children's privacy

Orbit Pop does not collect personal information through gameplay from users of any age. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, contact us so we can review and delete that information as appropriate.

7. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added cookies, advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts to this page. GitHub may process technical information about website visits, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Visiting this page is separate from playing Orbit Pop offline.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or our information practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised last-updated date. If a future version introduces data collection, advertising, or online features, we will update the policy and applicable Google Play disclosures and provide any notice or consent required.
